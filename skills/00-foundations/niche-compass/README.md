## How it works

1. **Interview.** Six short rounds: who you are, what you know, what you love, what people need, where the money is, and the cut: what you'll drop.
2. **Proposal.** One positioning line, three pillars, and a keep / drop / postpone table. You adjust until it feels like you.
3. **One Pager.** A single page you add to your Claude Project. Every future idea is checked against its filter: it's either yours or it isn't.

**What's in the One Pager**

| Section | Answers |
|:--|:--|
| Who · What · Why · Audience | Who you are, what you do, why you're here, who you talk to |
| 3 Pillars | The only topics you post about, and the content types under each |
| Idea filter | Three yes/no questions + real examples from your world |
| Money | A ladder from free to service, and the one step to focus on first |
| Keep · Drop · Postpone | What you gave up, and when to look at it again |
| Start tomorrow | 10 ideas that already pass the filter, and your first 3 posts in order |
| Bios | Instagram, TikTok, X and LinkedIn, counted to each limit |

## Get started

### 1 · Install (once)

1. **[Download the ZIP](https://github.com/adeltechtalks/AdelTechTalks-Claude/raw/main/downloads/niche-compass.zip)**.
2. In Claude, open **Customize → Skills**, click **+**, and upload the ZIP as it is (don't unzip it).

### 2 · Start the interview

```
I feel lost about what my content should be about. Interview me and build my One Pager.
```

Have a profile already? Add a screenshot of it, and the skill keeps your bio's style.

### 3 · Use it every day

Add `one-pager.json` (or the PDF) to your Claude Project's knowledge. Then, for any new idea:

```
Check this idea against my One Pager: [idea]
```
